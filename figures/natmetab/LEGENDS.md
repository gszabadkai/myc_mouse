# Figure legends (Nature Metabolism Letter)

Drafts for the Doc's "Nat metab version". The author's rules for these legends (2026-10-16): describe
what is on each panel and give what is needed to read it; no extra detail and no interpretation,
except the figure title and, where useful, a short sentence naming what a panel shows. Nature
Metabolism also asks for the centre value, error bars, *n*, the test and the *P* values, and for keys
in words, not symbols. Every number for the R panels (d–i) is printed by the panel's script; the bench
panels (a–c) are the author's Prism graphs and images, and their gaps are marked **[...]**.

## Extended Data Fig. 1

**Extended Data Fig. 1 | [Proposed title:] The MYC transcriptional programme halves in amplitude between 6 and 12 weeks without changing its composition.**
**a**, Expression of the MYC/MAX/MXD network genes, grouped as shown, in MYC+ versus WT MECs at 6W and 12W: log₂ fold change with 95% confidence interval (DESeq2 Wald test; *n* = 6 mice per group). No comparison reached an independent hypothesis weighting (IHW)-adjusted *P* < 0.05 (smallest, *P* = 0.061). **b**, MYC+ versus WT log₂ fold change at 12W against 6W for the 2,648 genes changed by MYC at 6W (IHW-adjusted *P* < 0.1, |log₂ fold change| ≥ 0.2, mean normalised count ≥ 20); solid line, least-squares fit through the origin; dashed line, equal effect at both ages; *R*², squared Pearson correlation. Twenty-two genes beyond ±3 are not shown and are included in the fit. *n* = 6 mice per group. **c**, Normalised enrichment scores of 866 gene sets (816 from the gene-set library and 50 MSigDB Hallmarks) for MYC+ versus WT at 6W (left) and 12W (right), from fGSEA on the DESeq2 Wald statistic; one point per gene set, grouped by programme. *n* = 6 mice per group. **d**, The MEC-specific switchable MYC model: MMTV-Cre and *Rosa26*-CAG-LSL-MYC-ER-IRES-eGFP (MYC<sup>ER</sup>) transgenes. **[Schematic created in BioRender: citation.]**

## Extended Data Fig. 2

Panel order follows the text (author, 2026-10-16). **d** is MYC's effect at 12W against 6W (script `edfig2e_myc_reallocation_ages.R`) and **e** the window against MYC (script `edfig2d_window_independent.R`): the reverse of the scripts' letters, which are left as they are. **f** is the MYC-effect ranking (script `edfig2f_reallocation_ranked.R`), **g** the window ranking (script `edfig2g_reallocation_window_ranked.R`), as the scripts are named. The first assembled figure (`_final_pdfs/NM_Extended_Data_Fig2.pdf`) has d/e in the scripts' order and f/g the other way round; both pairs are being swapped on the figure.

**Extended Data Fig. 2 | [Proposed title:] MYC and the pubertal–adult transition reshape mitochondrial pathway priorities independently.**
**a**, Design of the RNA-seq of purified MECs: WT and MYC+ mice at 6W and 12W (*n* = 6 mice per group); arrows, the contrasts of the DESeq2 model (age × genotype): MYC+ versus WT at each age, 12W versus 6W in each genotype, and their interaction. **b**, The gene-set library: number of gene sets per category (986 sets), coloured by mitochondrial content (MitoCarta3.0 sets; curated sets intersected with MitoCarta3.0; sets without this restriction). **c**, Principal component analysis of per-mouse gene-set scores (mean z-scored expression of each set's genes; 885 sets). Left, PC1 against PC2 (variance explained in parentheses); small dots, mice; large dots, group means; the PC1 axis is broken for one MYC+ 6W mouse. Right, absolute PC1 loading (correlation of each set's score with PC1) of the 885 sets, ranked and coloured as in **b**; selected sets are named. **d**, MYC+ versus WT priority (mitoPPS) at 12W against 6W for 143 nuclear MitoPathways; solid line, least-squares fit; dashed line, equal effect at both ages; black, the MitoPathways shown in Fig. 1e. **e**, Change in priority (mitoPPS) of 143 nuclear MitoPathways: WT 12W versus 6W against MYC+ versus WT at 6W (left), and MYC+ against WT 12W versus 6W (right); dashed line, equal change; *r*, Pearson correlation. **f**, The 143 nuclear MitoPathways ranked by the MYC+ versus WT change in priority (mitoPPS) at 6W (large dots), each joined to the 12W change (small dots). Colour, top-level MitoPathway of the MitoCarta3.0 hierarchy; ringed and named, the seven top-level MitoPathways themselves. Asterisks, Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted *P* < 0.05 at 6W (two-sided Welch's *t*-test on per-mouse scores, adjusted across MitoPathways). **g**, As in **f**, ranked by the WT change in priority from 6W to 12W (filled dots), each joined to the MYC+ change (open dots); asterisks, adjusted *P* < 0.05; no WT change reached it. In **d**–**g**, *n* = 6 mice per group.

## Extended Data Fig. 3

**Extended Data Fig. 3 | [Proposed title:] *Bbc3* and its regulator *Foxo3* fall further than the attenuation of the MYC programme predicts.**
**a**, Downstream apoptotic genes ranked by the departure (*z*) of their 12W MYC effect from the overall attenuation of MYC's effect, as in Fig. 1h. Fill, MYC+ versus WT log₂ fold change at 6W (*Htra2*, +1.49, and *Birc5*, +1.16, shown at the end of the scale). *Apaf1* (*z* = +2.14, empirical *P* = 0.018) is not shown. **b**, MYC+ versus WT change in BCL-2 family expression ratios (log₂) at 12W and at 6W multiplied by 0.43, the slope of a line through the origin relating the 12W to the 6W effects of the eight ratios other than *Bbc3*:*Bcl2l1* (bold). Filled, 6W *P* < 0.05; open, not significant (linear model of the log₂ ratio on age × genotype across 24 mice, genotype term at 6W, two-sided *t*-test, unadjusted); the 12W values were not tested. **c**, Regulators of PUMA ranked by the departure (*z*) of their 12W MYC effect from the overall attenuation of MYC's effect, as in Fig. 1h but signed by direction (negative, lower than predicted). Diamond, *Bbc3* (PUMA); circles, *Trp53* and p53 targets; triangles, other PUMA inducers; black, two-sided empirical *P* < 0.01 (values shown); grey, *P* > 0.05. *n* = 6 mice per group.

  as two separate batches, so each 12W-versus-6W change coincides with a batch; the genotype
