Two mouse experiments, two questions, and conflating them is the trap.

**MMTV-Myc 6W->12W is a DOSE experiment.** MYC protein falls ~50% at constant transcript (blot, 2026-07-25). That is the same number as script 40's x0.55 rescaling, so output per unit MYC is unchanged — which retrospectively explains every negative in the corpus (no reshaping in 40, no programme in excess in 34, no repressor in the 2026-07-25 scans, no moderating axis at Issue #5). There was nothing to find. The mito-protein and BAX/BIM/PUMA protein falls are the same x0.55 propagating to protein; they need no separate explanation.

**MYC-ER is a COMPETENCE experiment.** Acute induction at 12W: ~80% less death, only ~30% less proliferation, at equal or higher MYC protein. **Those animals had never seen MYC before induction**, so the substrate change cannot be MYC-driven selection — it must be developmental.

**THE COINCIDENCE MODEL (the author's, and I got this wrong once — do not repeat it).** "PGC1a is always there, Myc pushes it over the threshold at 6W" is **NOT a dose hypothesis**: the varying input is the SUBSTRATE (PGC1a/biogenesis falls developmentally), so **MYC-ER is its TEST, not its refutation** — it holds MYC fixed and varies exactly that input. Death needs MYC **and** a competent mitochondrial substrate. The frame earns its place three ways: proliferation needs one input and death two, so the 80%-vs-30% asymmetry follows with no extra assumption; a threshold on the product explains why PUMA priming collapses THROUGH zero rather than attenuating; and it is the manuscript's title thesis (mitochondria *integrating* two inputs) stated literally.

**The molecular hinge (provisional until `scripts/42_priming_arm_and_teb_substrate.R` is run).** MYC builds a death-ready mitochondrion at BOTH ages — Htra2 +1.49 (padj 2.6e-08, the strongest apoptotic gene here), Bax +0.48, Bcl-xL **down** -0.42, biogenesis up — and what it loses by 12W is the **trigger**. In one panel sharing the Bcl-xL denominator: **Bax:Bcl-xL retains 0.55 (the global rate, 46th pct) while PUMA:Bcl-xL retains -0.09 (92nd pct, interaction p 0.037; purity-adjusted p 0.006)**. The matched-pair null's own median retention is 0.53 — script 40's x0.55 rebuilt from random genes, i.e. a calibrated instrument. PUMA was **pre-specified** by the PGC1a cell experiments (which induce OXPHOS and PUMA "but not other BH3-only"). Headline the **contrast**, not the p-value: it contains its own control.

**Why PUMA, developmentally:** the Gray/ChEA shortlist lists Bbc3 as a MYC/MYCN/E2F1 target **only in the AP_TEB context**, "HS_TEB is apoptosis-loaded", and the TEB programme regresses in our WT data (MG_TEB_VS_DUCTAL_HS_GRAY_UP -0.45, p 0.0028; Aldh1a3 -2.22 padj 5.5e-5; Oxtr +1.72 padj 2.7e-4).

**THE NRF1->Bbc3 CITATION IS WRONG; THE AXIS IS NOT (2026-07-27).** Do NOT cite `Gray_et_al_developmental_TFS_selection.md`'s "the biogenesis TFs that build mito mass also touch pro-apoptotic effectors" — script 44 PART A shows that is the **base rate** (0.0284 pro per mito gene vs a catalog expectation of 0.0219 across 741 TFs; cor(regulon size, n_pro)=0.43), and the NRF1->Bbc3 row itself has **BH FDR 0.99998** with 5 mito genes, failing the resource's own gate. **The axis is real — the author has experimental evidence, and PGC1a induces PUMA AND BIM at protein level (WB).** Cite the blots. Also correct the record here: it is **PUMA and BIM**, not "PUMA but not other BH3-only". **But they SPLIT in vivo:** on script 44's genome-wide collapse statistic `Bbc3` is at the **0.51st percentile of 8774 Myc-responsive genes** while `Bcl2l11` is at the **91.6th** — so the pair does not transfer, and the mouse collapse is PUMA-specific. Report both; the failing half is what makes the surviving half worth quoting. See [[oxphos-apoptosis-module-verdict]].

**THE PRIORITY RULER IS THE STRONGEST IN-VIVO STRAND — do not forget it again.** mitoPPS is a pairwise RATIO, so a uniform x0.55 dose scaling CANCELS in it. The content ruler is dose-dominated; this one is not. And on it **OXPHOS is the most de-prioritised Level-1 tier on both temporal axes** (WT -0.069, Myc+ -0.134), carried by the structural SUBUNITS (-0.109/-0.153) while assembly factors do not move (-0.002/-0.029) — the subunits being the PGC1a/NRF1/ERRalpha output. So the arm PGC1a builds loses priority, and that is not dose. Script 38 PART B (already run) closes the loop: OXPHOS-ratio <-> priming-ratio design-adj Spearman **+0.70 vs a design-adj null of 0.48**, `redox` fails as the control, **neither circular nor ceiling-bound**. Three convergent strands = an INDICATION.

**EPISTEMIC CONTRACT for the whole death section:** the in-vivo transcriptome GENERATES the hypothesis; the cell perturbations PROVE it. Never let a correlation in these 24 mice stand where a causal claim belongs.

**Three negatives that must travel with it:** the per-sample OXPHOS<->PUMA coupling FAILS (at/below ambient) so the bundle is carried by the cell perturbations and never by in-vivo correlation; Bbc3 is NOT in the TEB-vs-ductal signature (the claim is TF-binding context, not expression); the MEC state composites are all n.s. Also excluded: anti-apoptotic buffering, p53/ARF, PUMA's known transcriptional inputs — which narrows the mechanism to the **inducibility of the pro-arm**. And there is no efferocytosis signature at 6W, so bulk RNA cannot see the death and *under-reports* the 6W induction (survivor bias) — the PUMA collapse is a lower bound.

**THE COINCIDENCE TEST, script 42 PART H (run 2026-07-25) — the best in-vivo support the model gets.** `lm(PUMA:Bcl-xL ~ myc * axis + epi + imm)`: **myc x OXPHOS-priority (mitoPPS ratio) = +2.79, p 0.005** — 3x the TEB axis, 8x the log-expression level axis; **the only axis that partially survives `tp*myc`** (+2.06, p 0.088, where TEB collapses 1.15 -> 0.53); within-timepoint permutation 92nd pct (p_emp 0.081); and **`redox` as negative control is null throughout** (p 0.72, permutation 51st pct). And the size objection dies in the right space: the WT developmental fall is 26% of the Myc effect on the LEVEL ruler but **97% of it on the PRIORITY ruler** where dose cancels. **The mediation/absorption test is UNUSABLE — its control failed** (redox "absorbs" 41%, more than any real axis); do not cite absorption in either direction. Still untested: PGC1a **ACTIVITY** (`Ppargc1a` baseMean ~30; coactivator activity is post-translational) — the author's model lives there and the cell perturbations are its evidence. Inversion to state in the paper: **in vivo BAX tracks mitochondrial mass (OXPHOS~BAX +0.66/+0.46, ~75-80th pct of ambient) and PUMA does not (+0.00/-0.40)** — the opposite of the cell result.

**Two corrections from the run, do not repeat them.** (1) PUMA's specificity is **GRADED, not binary**: Bmf (-0.83) and Bcl2l11 (-0.45) have more extreme retentions but non-significant 6W effects (p 0.41/0.46) — a retention is a ratio of noisy quantities, so you cannot lose an effect you never had. Among pairs with a real 6W effect (Bax 0.55, Bid 0.42, Bak1 0.27, PUMA **-0.09**, Bax:Mcl1 0.84) PUMA is the only one that REVERSES. (2) The AP_TEB finding is a **CONTEXT** statement, not TF specificity: Bbc3 sits in **71** AP_TEB regulons (TP53, ESR1, JUN, SOX2...) and in NO other context — broad promoter accessibility in one cell context. What survives: **MYC/E2F1 reach Bbc3 only in AP_TEB; elsewhere they reach Bid/Bok/Bak1/Pmaip1** — which BH3-only MYC can touch is context-dependent. Also: purity-adjusted interaction p is **0.017** (not 0.006), and Htra2 is a good internal control (attenuates LESS than matched genes, 37th pct).

**The decisive cheap experiment:** co-induce PGC1a in MYC-ER at 12W — the model predicts death is restored. Script 38 PART D already concluded this needs "an ESRRA/PGC1a perturbation vs the Myc substrate, not n=24 bulk"; that perturbation now exists in the cell arm.
