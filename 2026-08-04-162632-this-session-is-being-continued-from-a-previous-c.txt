  Three decisions:

  - It draws the contrast, not the level. Your sentence claims the gap, so the
  gap is on the axis. The levels form (figS8 panel B) needs ten facets and 183
  mm to say the same thing less directly.
  - No significance encoding, because nothing is significant — a key whose
  "filled" entry never appears on the panel is worse than one sentence in the
  legend.
